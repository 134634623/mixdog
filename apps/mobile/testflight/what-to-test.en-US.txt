Mixdog Mobile (beta) lets you continue your Mixdog desktop sessions from your phone.

How to test
1. On your PC, open Mixdog → Settings → Connection → Phone.
2. In the app, tap "Connect with a code" and enter the 6-digit code shown on your PC.
3. Approve the request on your PC.

Please check: the session list, reading and sending messages, and task-completion notifications.
